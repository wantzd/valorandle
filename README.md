# Valorandle

A daily guessing game for VALORANT esports fans. A fresh challenge every day across five modes — each with its own streak to keep alive.

**[valorandle.com](https://valorandle.com/en)** · Português & English

---

## How the tiles work

Most modes compare your guess against a hidden answer and colour each attribute:

- 🟩 **Green** — exact match
- 🟨 **Yellow** — close (within range, same group, or same region)
- 🟥 **Red** — no match

Arrows (↑ / ↓) on number attributes tell you whether the answer is higher or lower than your guess.

---

## Streak vs. Free

Every mode opens with a choice:

- **Streak** — the daily challenge. One puzzle per day; solving it keeps your streak for that mode alive. Each mode tracks its own streak.
- **Free** — practice mode. Play as many rounds as you want; it doesn't count toward your streak.

A new daily unlocks every day at midnight (UTC−3). The countdown in the top bar shows how long until the next one.

---

## The modes

### 🎯 Pro Players
Guess the hidden VCT pro player. Pick a league (Americas), then identify the daily's five players — one per round, up to 8 attempts each. Tiles compare **country, team, age, role, titles won, and years active**.

### 🔊 Skins
A weapon skin's inspection audio plays — name the skin from the sound. Each wrong guess reveals another clue (kill sound, then muted video). Tiles compare **bundle, weapon, edition, and act**.

### 🕵️ Agents
Identify the daily agent. Tiles compare **gender, role, origin, release year, and ultimate cost**. The confirmed clues stack up as you narrow it down.

### ⚡ Abilities
Two ways to play:
- **Description** — the ability's text is revealed one word at a time; name it before the description fills in. Unlimited guesses.
- **Image** — the ability icon is uncovered tile by tile with each wrong guess. Up to 8 attempts.

Tiles compare the **agent, ability slot, and whether it's the ultimate**.

### 🗺️ Maps
A zoomed-in screenshot of a map callout is shown. Choose the map, then click the callout on the minimap. The image zooms out with each wrong guess, and hints unlock as you go. Tiles compare **map, callout, and area**.

---

## Tips

- Turn on **Hard mode** (Pro Players) to hide the ↑ / ↓ arrows for an extra challenge.
- Use the **sound toggle** in each mode's status bar to mute feedback sounds.
- Switch between **Português** and **English** with the globe button in the top-right — your progress carries over.
- Finished a daily? **Share** your result grid, or come back tomorrow to keep the streak going.
